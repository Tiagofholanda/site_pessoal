Services

I work across the three fronts that usually show up together in a territorial project: the database, the application that queries it and the survey that feeds it.

WebGIS platforms and geoportals

Map applications for querying, editing and publishing data. React and Mapbox GL on the interface, GeoServer and GeoNode for layer publishing, access control, thematic filters and reports for technical teams.

Spatial databases

Modelling and maintenance of PostgreSQL/PostGIS bases: bulk import, validation rules, consistency audits, cross-database sync and automation in Python and R.

Survey and remote sensing

Drone photogrammetry with GNSS support (RTK/PPK), processing in Metashape and Pix4D, orthomosaics, DTM/DSM, volume calculation and technical review of aerial survey campaigns.

How I run a project

Order matters. An application built on a disorganised base becomes rework within months.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    I map which data already exists, how it is collected today and who uses each piece of information — an honest picture of what is ready and what is missing.

  2. 02

    Data structure

    I define the PostGIS model, validation rules and import flow, including the change history required for auditing.

  3. 03

    Application

    I build the WebGIS, dashboard or automation routine on top of that base, scoped to what the team actually uses day to day.

  4. 04

    Operation and handover

    I publish, document and train the team. The goal is for the client to keep operating without depending on me for routine tasks.
